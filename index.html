Torah Shorts Uploader

Torah Shorts Uploader (Google Cloud app name: "Bible uploader") is a small private program used by the owner of the Torah Shorts YouTube channel.

It uploads the channel's own videos (the weekly "The Bible Report" Shorts about the Torah portion) to that channel through the YouTube Data API, and sets each video's scheduled publish time.

It is not offered to the public and is used only by the channel owner.
